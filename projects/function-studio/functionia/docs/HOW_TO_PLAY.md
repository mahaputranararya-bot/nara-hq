# How to Play Functionia (and use it for Term 3)

## Start
Double-click `index.html` (Chrome or Edge). Click **New game**, pick a save slot and type your name if you like. No internet and no sign-up are needed.

## The goal
The Glitch shattered the Grand Graph. You are **Ori**, a living point who always knows its coordinates. With **Delta** the triangle bird, restore all six kingdoms by fixing their function rules. Then find out what The Glitch really is.

## Controls
| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Enter or Space | Launch (Ori rides your graph) |
| R | Instant retry (keeps your answers) |
| H | Ask Delta for a hint |
| J | Open the Journal |
| Esc | Pause menu, or skip a story scene |
| Tab | Move between boxes and buttons |
| Mouse wheel | Zoom the graph |

Type answers as whole numbers, decimals or fractions: `0.5`, `.5` and `1/2` are the same. Negatives work with `-`.

## How a puzzle works
1. Read the broken rule.
2. Set the function (type the numbers, or click the graph).
3. Press **Launch** and watch Ori ride the graph.
4. Hit the target and that piece of the kingdom is rebuilt for good. Miss, and you see your graph next to the target, plus Delta's tip.

Each kingdom has four stages: **Learn** (Delta shows a demo first), **Practice** (fresh numbers every time), the **Ruler's Trial** (no preview: predict first!) and the **Glitch Zone** (try strange inputs and break the model to find a Glitch Shard).

**Stars** come from hints and tries, never speed. 3 ★ = no hints and correct within 2 tries. 1 ★ is always enough to keep going. If you're stuck, Delta's hints go from a nudge, to the method, to a worked example, and finally "Show me the answer and explain it".

## Kingdoms and Term 3 topics
| Kingdom | Topic | You practise |
|---|---|---|
| Mirrorvale | Functions & inverses | vertical line test, reflecting across y = x, f(g(x)), inverses, domain & range |
| Lineara | Linear functions | slope m = rise ÷ run, y = mx + b, tables, cost models |
| Parabolis | Quadratics | vertex form, axis of symmetry, roots, completing the square |
| Cubrion | Cubics | origin symmetry, roots, end behaviour, turning points |
| Expona | Exponentials | y = a·bˣ, growth vs decay, the asymptote y = 0 |
| Rationalis | Rational functions | asymptotes, domain, range, holes |

Mirrorvale comes first. Then the middle four open in **any order you like**. Rationalis opens when the other five are restored.

## Revising for Term 3
- **Practice Mode** (map → Practice Mode): pick any restored kingdom and a topic, and get unlimited fresh puzzles. Tick "Mix ALL restored kingdoms" for exam-style mixed revision.
- **The Journal** (J): each restored kingdom adds a page with the rule, a key graph, key words, a worked example and the Glitch Shard's lesson. Press **Print all notes** for a paper copy.
- **Puzzle of the Day**: one mixed puzzle per day. It's optional, with no streaks, and missed days don't matter.

## Play with friends
- **Challenge codes:** every Practice puzzle shows a code like `PAR-3-8K2Q`. A classmate types it into **Challenge code** and gets the exact same puzzle. Then compare how you solved it.
- **Class Challenge:** the teacher clicks **Make a code** and shows it on the board. Everyone joins with that code and solves the same puzzle. Results show hints and tries, never speed.
- **3 save slots** let classmates share one laptop.

## Teacher mode
Open `index.html?dev=1`, then press Esc → **Dev panel** to unlock everything, jump to any stage and show the answers.
