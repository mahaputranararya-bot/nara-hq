Functionia

How to Play & how to use it for Term 3: Functions  •  Group 5  •  Mr. Darwin's Function Studio

The goal

The Glitch shattered the Grand Graph. You are Ori, a living point that always shows its coordinates. With Delta the triangle bird, restore six kingdoms by fixing their function rules, collect the six Glitch Shards, and discover who The Glitch really is.

Start: double-click index.html → New game. No internet, no sign-up.

Controls

Enter / SpaceLaunch: Ori rides your graph
RInstant retry (keeps your answers)
HHint from Delta
JJournal (your revision notes)
EscPause, or skip a story scene

Answers: 0.5 = .5 = 1/2. Negatives with -.

Every puzzle in 4 steps

  1. Read the broken rule.
  2. Set the function: type the numbers, or click the graph.
  3. Launch and watch Ori ride the graph.
  4. Hit the target and that piece is rebuilt for good. Miss, and you see your graph next to the target and get a tip.

Each kingdom: Learn (demo first) → Practice (new numbers every time) → Ruler's Trial (no preview: predict first!) → Glitch Zone (break the model on purpose to win a shard). Stars come from hints and tries, never speed. 1 ★ is always enough to keep going.

Revise for Term 3

  • Practice Mode (on the map): unlimited fresh puzzles from any restored kingdom, or mix them all for exam revision.
  • Journal (J): rule, key graph, key words and a worked example per kingdom. Use Print all notes.
  • Puzzle of the Day: one mixed puzzle a day. Optional, with no streaks.

Challenge your friends

  • Every Practice puzzle has a challenge code like PAR-3-8K2Q. A friend types it in and gets the same puzzle. Then compare methods.
  • Class Challenge: the teacher makes one code and everyone solves it together. Results show hints and tries, never speed.
  • 3 save slots, so you can share a laptop.
