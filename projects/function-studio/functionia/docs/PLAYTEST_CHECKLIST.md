# Functionia — Peer Play-Test Checklist (2+ other crews)

## Before the session
- [ ] Copy the `functionia` folder to each laptop (USB is fine). Double-click `index.html` to check it opens with no internet.
- [ ] Turn the volume to a sensible level. Have paper copies of `docs/how-to-play-card.html` and the feedback form below.
- [ ] Use a **fresh save slot** for each tester. Slot 3 can be reset in Settings, which takes two clicks.
- [ ] Decide who watches and who takes notes. **Don't help unless the tester is stuck for more than 2 minutes.**

## What to watch for (write down the time)
- [ ] **First 5 minutes:** does the tester understand the goal? How long until the first win (the bridge)? Did they skip the story?
- [ ] **The core loop:** do they see what Launch does? Do they read Delta's feedback after a miss, or just guess again?
- [ ] **Confusion:** any moment they say "what do I do now?" Note the screen and the puzzle name.
- [ ] **Hints:** do they find the Hint button? Do the hints actually help?
- [ ] **Typing answers:** any answer they thought was right but was marked wrong? Write down exactly what they typed.
- [ ] **Glitches:** do they enjoy "You broke the model!" or find it scary or annoying?
- [ ] **Choice:** after Mirrorvale, which kingdom did they pick and why?
- [ ] **Fun moments:** smiles, "ooh", laughs. What caused them?
- [ ] **Bugs:** anything frozen, overlapping, unreadable or broken. Take a phone photo.
- [ ] **Stopping:** did they stop at a Kingdom Restored screen? Did it feel like a natural break?

## Questions to ask after (2–3 minutes)
1. In one sentence, what is the game about?
2. What is one math idea you understand better now?
3. Where did you get stuck, and what would have helped?
4. Which character did you like most? Why?
5. Would you use Practice Mode to revise for a test? Why or why not?
6. If you could change one thing, what would it be?

## After the session (crew)
- [ ] Put the scores in a table and find the average fun and clarity.
- [ ] List the top 3 stuck points and decide a fix for each (better hint, clearer prompt or easier numbers).
- [ ] If a correct answer was marked wrong, tell Uno (Math Anchor) and Nara (code) straight away. Add that input to `tests/parser.test.js`.
- [ ] Write what you changed in `docs/PROGRESS.md`.
